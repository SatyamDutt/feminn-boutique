About the Boutique

A Ludhiana destination for considered fashion

Feminn Boutique is a women’s boutique in Civil Lines, Ludhiana, known for fashion and bridal-wear styling, readymade garments, fabrics and embroidery work. Established in 1989, the boutique serves customers from Ludhiana and the surrounding region.

Located near Green Florist on College Road, Feminn Boutique brings together clothing, fabrics and styling guidance in one accessible location. Whether you are looking for an everyday outfit, festive wear or an occasion look, the boutique offers a space to explore styles at your own pace.
